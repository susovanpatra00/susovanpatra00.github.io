About

A bit about me

I'm a Voice AI engineer. Most of my work is getting speech recognition, an LLM and text-to-speech to run together quickly enough for a phone call, and getting them to handle Indian languages and accents properly.

Before that I worked on enterprise RAG and document tools. I did my M.Tech in Computer Science at IIT Kanpur (2022–24), where my research was GPS-free positioning from phone sensors.

Outside work I write Triton kernels for Qwen models and build computer vision tools for site safety. Those are under Projects below.

GPU / Inference

qwen2-triton-kernels

Fused Triton kernels for RMSNorm, SwiGLU and attention that patch into any Qwen2.5 model. Up to 26x faster in isolation, and about 1.1x faster end to end on the 7B and 14B models (A100). The 0.5B model sees no gain because matmuls dominate there.
